About Teach Hub

Teach Hub is the official support and work-submission app for our teaching program. We've been running for 3 years, helping students stay in direct contact with their instructors.

Through this app you can chat with your instructor, ask questions, and submit your work (photos, screenshots, or voice notes) directly - all in one place.

This is the genuine, official Teach Hub app. If you ever have a question about anything, you can always ask your instructor right here in the chat.

To install this app on your home screen:

Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right of your browser) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
